## 0. Purpose

This document defines how the Polynovea public website should explain the company, organise its portfolio, build a distinctive brand, attract people who have never heard of Polynovea, and become understandable to both humans and search / answer / generative systems.

The central problem is simple:

> **Polynovea cannot rely on Polynovea vocabulary to create Polynovea discovery.**

Terms such as **Polynovea**, **Infrakinetic**, **HBIF**, and **Content Operations Platform** can become valuable branded concepts over time, but they are not sufficient acquisition language for people who do not already know what the company is.

The website therefore needs two simultaneous language layers:

1. **Familiar category, problem and outcome language** — how an unfamiliar buyer, developer, researcher or search engine recognises relevance.
2. **Proprietary Polynovea language** — how the company builds differentiation and memory once relevance is established.

Working rule:

> **Category and problem language gets the visitor through the door. Polynovea language gives them a reason to remember us.**

This strategy is grounded in current Polynovea source documents, the current website, marketing science, usability research, search-engine guidance, open-source product benchmarks, and current research on generative-engine visibility.

# 1. Source-of-truth boundaries

The website must reflect the company's actual architecture rather than inventing a cleaner but false public story.

## 1.1 Polynovea

Polynovea is the parent company. Current internal doctrine describes it as a deep-tech AI, behavioural-intelligence and decision-infrastructure company building commercially independent domain operating systems around a longer-horizon behavioural research architecture.

For public communication, that wording should be translated into language an unfamiliar audience can understand without deleting the deeper positioning.

## 1.2 Domain products

**Products** are domain-specific commercial product surfaces.

Current public/product state:

- **Infrakinetic** — Workplace / organisational operating environment; current commercial lead.
- **Hospitality** — remains a domain product area but is under architectural rebuild. Historical Decision Framework / Acquisition / Optimisation work is lineage and evidence, not the future product specification.

The company rule remains:

> Layer 1 remains domain-specific. Cross-domain intelligence is earned through evidence, not declared.

## 1.3 Open source

The Polynovea CMS / Content Operations Platform is **not a domain-specific Layer-1 product and must not be placed in the Products directory as if it were one**.

It belongs under a separate first-class **Open Source** surface.

Current source documentation describes it as an open-source, self-hostable Content Operations Platform combining structured data, content creation, collaboration, review, releases, delivery operations, infrastructure control and operational intelligence.

Its familiar-category descriptors include:

- open-source CMS;
- headless CMS;
- self-hosted CMS;
- structured-content platform;
- visual schema / data modelling environment;
- developer-automatable content platform;
- governed content operations platform.

The proprietary phrase **Content Operations Platform** should remain, but should not be the only phrase asked to carry discovery.

## 1.4 HBIF / Research

HBIF is a research architecture and programme, not another SaaS product card.

Public claims must preserve current evidence boundaries:

- Layer 1 is domain-specific.
- Layer 2 studies shared behavioural state and dynamics; BPM is the current technical reference.
- Layer 3 is evidence-gated cross-domain / frontier research.
- Cross-domain validity is not currently established as universal.
- Research status, implementation status, deployment status and commercial availability are different things.

## 1.5 Data and research boundary

Existing doctrine is non-negotiable:

> Standard product use does not automatically grant unrestricted HBIF research rights.

Research participation must be separately permissioned and governed.

### New founder direction recorded 2026-09-19

The future **managed cloud edition of the open-source platform is intended to create a path for gathering permissioned evidence/data that can contribute to HBIF Layer 3 research**.

This is a **future strategic direction**, not a current public product capability.

It should eventually be implemented as two clearly separable modes:

**A. Standard managed cloud**  
A normal hosted product. Customers receive the managed service and are not automatically research participants.

**B. Research Partner / permissioned data-lab mode**  
A separately contracted and consented programme where defined evidence classes may be used for defined research purposes under provenance, minimisation, retention, deletion, governance and access rules.

The self-hosted OSS edition must never imply that it phones home or silently contributes research data.

# 2. Recommended public information architecture

The website should expose the company through clear human categories rather than mirroring internal architecture diagrams.

```text
Polynovea
├── Home
├── Products
│   ├── Infrakinetic / Workplace
│   └── Hospitality / rebuild
├── Open Source
│   ├── Content Operations Platform / CMS
│   └── Future OSS projects
├── HBIF
│   └── Architecture
├── Research
│   ├── Research programme
│   ├── Experiments / findings / papers
│   └── Domain studies
├── Insights
├── Company
└── Contact
```

### Why this separation matters

**Products** answers: *What can Polynovea commercialise for a particular operating domain?*  
**Open Source** answers: *What infrastructure/software does Polynovea release for people to inspect, self-host, use and contribute to?*  
**HBIF / Research** answers: *What is Polynovea investigating scientifically?*  
**Company** answers: *Who is Polynovea, why does it exist, and how does it work?*

This prevents the current taxonomy error where the Content Operations Platform appears as if it were another domain product beside Workplace and Hospitality.

# 3. Marketing-science strategy

## 3.1 Mental availability before internal elegance

Ehrenberg-Bass research frames **Category Entry Points (CEPs)** as the thoughts buyers have as they move into a category purchase. These are building blocks of mental availability.

For Polynovea, the website should therefore be organised around situations that cause someone to look for an answer — not merely around the terms Polynovea has invented.

### Example CEP hypotheses for Infrakinetic

These are hypotheses to validate with customers/search data, not finished copy:

- “We have too many disconnected business tools.”
- “Sales, billing and finance do not share context.”
- “Approvals live in email or WhatsApp.”
- “We are outgrowing spreadsheets.”
- “Our CRM stops at the deal and operations starts somewhere else.”
- “We want fewer SaaS tools.”
- “We need one place for sales, finance, people and operations.”
- “We need better executive visibility across departments.”
- “We are replacing a patchwork of CRM, HR, billing and workflow tools.”

### Example CEP hypotheses for Open Source

- “We need an open-source headless CMS.”
- “We need to self-host our CMS.”
- “We need structured content and APIs, not only pages.”
- “Our editorial workflow is disconnected from releases and delivery.”
- “We need a CMS our developers can inspect and automate.”
- “We need visual schema modelling without giving up database discipline.”
- “We need content governance and approval.”
- “We want control of our data and deployment.”

### Example CEP hypotheses for HBIF / Research

- “How can human behaviour be modelled mathematically without assuming determinism?”
- “How do behavioural states change over time?”
- “Can behavioural mechanisms transfer across domains?”
- “Can AI systems use behavioural state with provenance and uncertainty?”
- “How should human decision models preserve contradictory evidence?”

These are much stronger discovery surfaces than expecting an unfamiliar audience to search “HBIF”.

## 3.2 Distinctive brand assets

Ehrenberg-Bass distinguishes brand assets that are famous and unique enough to trigger the brand without the name. The implication for Polynovea is not “redesign frequently”; it is the opposite: identify a small set of assets and build them consistently.

Candidate Polynovea distinctive assets:

- the gold Polynovea mark;
- black / near-black field;
- violet / deep-purple spatial environment;
- gold as an authority / signal accent rather than a generic decorative colour;
- neural / node / connection line language;
- controlled spatial depth and kinetic transitions;
- consistent display typography;
- the phrase **Build evidence faster than narrative** as a company doctrine where contextually appropriate.

Do not change these merely to resemble another AI startup. Improve their usability and consistency instead.

## 3.3 Portfolio cohesion without portfolio confusion

The company should look related across Products, Open Source and Research, but each surface should carry a different information character.

Recommended:

- **Products:** operational, concrete, workflow/evidence oriented.
- **Open Source:** inspectable, technical, modular, code/data ownership oriented.
- **Research:** quieter, rigorous, publication/evidence oriented.
- **Company:** synthesis of all three.

Same parent visual DNA; different semantic emphasis.

# 4. Brand positioning architecture

## 4.1 What the website should not do

Avoid reducing Polynovea to any one of these:

- “AI startup”;
- “behavioural analytics company”;
- “CRM company”;
- “ERP company”;
- “HBIF company”;
- “CMS company”.

All are incomplete and some create category confusion.

## 4.2 Working positioning territory

The strongest current territory is the intersection of:

- business / organisational operating software;
- governed decision infrastructure;
- open technical infrastructure;
- behavioural and decision research.

### Company positioning hypotheses to test

Do not treat these as final copy yet:

**Hypothesis A**  
> Polynovea builds software for complex operations and researches how decisions, behaviour and outcomes change over time.

**Hypothesis B**  
> Enterprise software, open infrastructure and behavioural research — built around evidence, governance and connected decisions.

**Hypothesis C**  
> We build systems for how organisations operate — and research how decisions happen.

The final home hero should be tested against comprehension, credibility and memorability, not just aesthetic preference.

## 4.3 Message ladder

A visitor should be able to understand Polynovea at progressively deeper levels.

### Level 1 — recognition

“What kind of company is this?”

Use familiar words.

### Level 2 — relevance

“What does it solve / build for someone like me?”

Use problems, workflows and outcomes.

### Level 3 — differentiation

“What is unusual about how Polynovea approaches this?”

Introduce evidence, governance, connected state, product architecture, open infrastructure and HBIF.

### Level 4 — technical depth

“Can I inspect the claims?”

Expose architecture, implementation boundaries, research status, documentation, experiments, code and evidence where public.

Polynovea should not ask a first-time visitor to begin at Level 4.

# 5. Copy principles

## 5.1 Familiar phrase first, proprietary phrase second

Bad discovery pattern:

> Content Operations Platform for governed operational intelligence.

Better pattern:

> **An open-source, self-hostable headless CMS and structured-content platform.**  
> Polynovea’s Content Operations Platform connects schemas, editorial workflows, releases, APIs and delivery operations in one governed system.

Bad discovery pattern:

> Human Behavioural Intelligence Framework.

Better pattern:

> **Research into computational models of human behaviour and decision-making.**  
> HBIF is Polynovea’s evidence-gated research framework for behavioural state, dynamics and decision mechanisms.

## 5.2 Product pages should describe jobs, not inventories

For Infrakinetic, the internal engine inventory is not the opening pitch.

The source guide provides a stronger framing:

> sell → contract → deliver → bill → collect → account → recruit → hire → onboard → pay → approve → govern → serve customers

Use this to create workflow-led sections and search landing content.

## 5.3 Evidence beats adjectives

Reduce words such as:

- revolutionary;
- intelligent;
- next-generation;
- seamless;
- cutting-edge;
- transformative;
- world-class.

Use evidence instead:

- what the system connects;
- what remains separately owned;
- what has been implemented;
- what has been deployed;
- what has been tested;
- what is still research;
- what a user can actually do.

This aligns with the company rule: **Build evidence faster than narrative.**

## 5.4 Search copy must still sound human

Do not force exact-match keyword repetition. Google's language systems understand related phrasing; use the important category terms naturally in title, H1, introductory text, descriptive links and supporting sections.

# 6. Design strategy

## 6.1 Preserve Polynovea; remove friction

The current dark spatial/neural identity is more distinctive than a generic white SaaS template. The redesign should therefore preserve the identity while improving comprehension.

Do not imitate Anthropic, Palantir, Supabase, Strapi or Payload aesthetically. Borrow useful information-architecture patterns, not their skins.

## 6.2 First viewport rule

The first viewport must establish, without scrolling:

1. what Polynovea is;
2. why the visitor might care;
3. the primary next actions.

A visually impressive first screen with weak information scent fails both usability and discovery.

## 6.3 Motion rule

Motion should communicate:

- hierarchy;
- state transition;
- relationship;
- depth;
- system behaviour.

It should not:

- block text;
- delay navigation;
- require animation to understand meaning;
- cause section collisions;
- create expensive GPU work without communication value.

`prefers-reduced-motion` and mobile flow should remain first-class experiences.

## 6.4 Information density

Use progressive disclosure:

- direct headline;
- one-paragraph explanation;
- proof / status;
- visual model;
- deeper technical detail.

Do not put the complete company ontology in the hero.

## 6.5 Visual proof components to build

Instead of decorative feature grids everywhere, build reusable proof components:

- **Status badge:** Research / Implemented / Phase complete / Rebuild / Upcoming OSS.
- **Evidence card:** claim → evidence type → date / boundary.
- **Workflow chain:** e.g. lead → agreement → delivery → invoice → payment → finance.
- **Architecture boundary diagram.**
- **Research status panel:** hypothesis / experiment / result / boundary.
- **OSS control panel:** self-hosting, portability, governance, APIs, deployment choice.
- **Comparison / migration matrix** when evidence supports it.

These components become both brand language and AEO/GEO-friendly information structures.

# 7. SEO strategy

## 7.1 Core SEO principle

The site currently over-indexes on branded and invented phrases. SEO must expand from **brand terms** to **category + problem + workflow + outcome + comparison + educational intent**.

Google explicitly advises anticipating the words different readers use to search. Expert and novice terminology can differ.

## 7.2 Query architecture

Every important surface should map to a search-intent family.

```text
Problem intent
   ↓
Category intent
   ↓
Capability / workflow intent
   ↓
Evaluation / comparison intent
   ↓
Brand / product intent
```

Polynovea currently starts too far down that chain.

# 9. AEO — Answer Engine Optimisation

AEO should not become a pile of FAQ schema.

Google removed FAQ rich results in 2026. Visible FAQ content can still help readers and answer systems, but FAQ markup is no longer a Google rich-result growth tactic.

## 9.1 Answer-first content pattern

For major questions, give a direct answer near the beginning:

**What is Infrakinetic?**  
One direct sentence, then detail.

**What is HBIF?**  
One direct sentence, then layers/evidence status.

**Is the Polynovea CMS open source?**  
Direct status answer, then release-state caveat.

**Can I self-host it?**  
Direct answer tied to actual release state.

**Does using Polynovea software send data into HBIF?**  
Direct “No, not automatically,” followed by the permissioned research model.

## 9.2 Make answers extractable

Use:

- descriptive H2/H3 questions;
- short direct opening paragraphs;
- definition lists;
- real tables where comparisons are appropriate;
- explicit status labels;
- dates/version context for changing claims;
- links to deeper evidence;
- consistent entity naming.

# 10. GEO — Generative Engine Optimisation

## 10.1 Treat GEO as retrieval + selection + citation, not a trick

The original GEO research showed content-presentation strategies could improve visibility in its benchmark, with effects varying by domain. That is useful evidence, but current generative systems are stochastic and platform-specific; no single markup trick guarantees organic citation.

Polynovea should optimise for being a **useful source**, not for “gaming AI”.

## 10.2 What makes Polynovea cite-worthy

The company has unusually strong potential material if published carefully:

- original research questions;
- experimental methods;
- negative / null findings;
- architecture reasoning;
- product design decisions;
- implementation boundaries;
- original diagrams;
- release evidence;
- open-source code;
- measured performance;
- founder/engineering explanations based on first-hand work.

Generic “10 AI trends” articles are weak GEO assets because they add little unique information.

## 10.3 Google-specific reality

Google states that its AI Overviews / AI Mode require no special AI markup beyond normal Search eligibility and SEO fundamentals. Important content should be textual, crawlable, internally linked, useful and consistent with structured data.

`llms.txt` may be kept for systems that choose to consume it, but Google clarified in June 2026 that it does not positively or negatively affect Google Search visibility/ranking.

## 10.4 GEO content form

High-value pages should contain:

- a clear definition;
- explicit scope;
- concrete evidence;
- a date/version;
- named authors/organisation;
- limitations;
- original examples or data;
- references when using external work;
- a stable canonical URL.

# 13. Competitor / benchmark lessons

These are information-architecture lessons, not aesthetic templates.

## Strapi

Strapi leads with familiar language: open-source headless CMS / content framework. It makes self-hosting and managed Strapi Cloud explicit deployment choices. The lesson is to **name the category before expanding it**.

## Payload

Payload says “open-source Next.js backend” and then explains use cases such as headless CMS. Its “code-first for developers, content-first for marketers” pattern demonstrates clear role-based information scent.

## Supabase

Supabase clearly distinguishes its hosted platform from self-hosting and documents the responsibility shift. The lesson is deployment clarity, not copying its product language.

## Anthropic

Anthropic gives research a dedicated first-class surface with named research areas. The lesson is to separate research from product navigation rather than presenting research as another product card.

# 14. Technical / performance strategy

Polynovea's visual system uses WebGL and motion, so performance must be managed as part of brand quality.

Current Core Web Vitals good thresholds:

- LCP ≤ 2.5 s;
- INP ≤ 200 ms;
- CLS ≤ 0.1;
- evaluate at the 75th percentile, mobile and desktop.

Website upgrade work should therefore include:

- field measurement, not Lighthouse-only assumptions;
- lazy/non-blocking 3D work where possible;
- no content dependency on WebGL success;
- static/textual semantic DOM for important claims;
- sensible image formats and dimensions;
- stable layout dimensions;
- motion reduction path;
- mobile-first performance budget.

The visual identity is valuable only if users can receive it quickly and reliably.

## 17.1 Current external benchmark findings — September 2026

This pass adds a stronger conclusion from current category leaders, usability research and current search-engine guidance.

### Finding A — category language and proprietary positioning should coexist

The most useful benchmark is not that successful companies avoid proprietary language. They often create it. The pattern is that they **anchor the proprietary idea in a category the visitor already recognises**.

- Zoho One uses the proprietary proposition **“The Operating System for Business”**, but immediately explains it through growing businesses, customers, employees, finances and operations on a unified system.
- Microsoft Dynamics 365 leads with the established categories **ERP and CRM applications**, then explains the broader value as connecting teams, processes and data.
- Strapi currently calls itself an **Open-Source Content Framework**, while repeatedly retaining the familiar **open-source headless CMS** category and making self-hosted versus managed cloud explicit.
- Contentful calls itself a **content platform** but states that a **headless CMS** sits at its core.
- Sanity has deliberately built the branded category **Content Operating System**, but still explains it through structured content, content database, APIs, SDKs, Studio and content operations.

**Implication for Polynovea:** do not replace “Content Operations Platform”, “Infrakinetic”, “HBIF” or other ownable concepts. Pair them with the category/problem language people already understand. Proprietary language should create differentiation after relevance, not carry discovery alone.

### Finding B — the Open Source / Cloud split is strategically correct

Strapi provides a particularly relevant benchmark: the same open-source project can be self-hosted on the customer's infrastructure or deployed to a vendor-managed cloud. The user-facing distinction is explicit: ownership/control when self-hosted, operational convenience when managed.

**Implication for Polynovea:** `/open-source` should establish software ownership, self-hosting, inspectability and portability first. Polynovea Cloud should be presented later as a managed deployment/service layer rather than as the reason the OSS exists. Any future Research Partner mode must be a third, separately permissioned relationship rather than an implied property of cloud hosting.

### Finding C — search and generative discovery are converging around the same fundamentals

Google's 2026 generative-AI guidance explicitly says foundational SEO remains the basis for AI visibility. It emphasizes unique, non-commodity, expert/first-hand content, crawlability, clear structure, textual availability, internal linking and a good page experience. It also warns against creating many pages merely to capture every fan-out query.

Bing's 2026 AI Performance tooling measures citations, cited URLs and grounding queries across AI answers. Bing's own improvement guidance emphasizes subject depth, clear headings/tables, evidence, current information and reduced ambiguity.

**Implication for Polynovea:** SEO, AEO and GEO should share one content/evidence system rather than three disconnected content programmes. The best Polynovea GEO material is likely to be original research, technical evidence, architecture decisions, public experiments, release/certification evidence and useful first-hand product lessons — not generic AI-written topic pages.

### Finding D — credibility is part of the design system

Stanford's web-credibility research highlights verifiability, evidence, visible organisational reality, expertise, contactability, professional design, usability, current content and restraint in promotional language. These principles align unusually well with Polynovea's existing “Build evidence faster than narrative” doctrine.

**Implication for Polynovea:** the redesign should visibly expose proof rather than hide it in long prose. Product state, evidence type, research status, authorship, version/date, source links and limitations should become recurring interface components.

### Finding E — unfamiliar internal vocabulary is a UX problem before it is an SEO problem

Nielsen Norman Group's “match between system and the real world” heuristic says interfaces should use words and concepts familiar to users rather than system-oriented terminology; it also notes that familiar words are better for SEO because they are the words people search. Information-scent research similarly shows that people choose links based on labels, surrounding context and prior knowledge.

**Implication for Polynovea:** primary navigation labels should remain literal — Products, Open Source, Research, Company — while proprietary language belongs one level deeper. A visitor should never need to understand the company ontology to predict where a navigation item goes.

### Finding F — accessibility and performance are brand constraints, not compliance afterthoughts

WCAG 2.2 requires headings and labels to describe topic/purpose and visible keyboard focus for operable controls. For a visually ambitious deep-tech site, accessible hierarchy, focus states, contrast and reduced-motion behaviour are part of perceived quality.

**Implication for Polynovea:** the visual identity can remain spatial, dark and kinetic, but the semantic/text layer must remain complete without WebGL or motion. Every major interaction must have a usable keyboard/focus state, and motion cannot be required to discover meaning.

### Finding G — current competitor language creates a positioning opportunity for Infrakinetic

Zoho One and Microsoft Dynamics demonstrate that broad operational suites are normally explained through known business jobs and known categories: customers, employees, finance, operations, ERP, CRM, sales and service. This reinforces the decision not to make “enterprise operating system” the only acquisition term.

For Infrakinetic, the stronger public-language stack is therefore:

```text
problem / workflow language
→ familiar software category language
→ Infrakinetic brand
→ “Where Business Operates as One” differentiation
→ technical architecture / evidence
```

The site should be able to answer ordinary searches such as business operations software, all-in-one business software, CRM + billing/finance, workflow/approval software and ERP alternatives **without falsely claiming feature parity with every ERP category**.

### Finding H — do not use meta keywords as a strategy

Keyword research remains essential for understanding demand and language, but a `keywords` meta field is not the mechanism that earns Google rankings. Page purpose, useful copy, titles/headings, internal links, crawlability, entities, evidence and user value are the implementation layer that matters.

This means the future quantitative keyword sheet should drive **information architecture and content decisions**, not a giant invisible tag list.

# 22. UI / UX and visual-design research

The earlier strategy established information architecture and discovery. This section addresses a different question:

> **What should Polynovea feel like before a visitor has read enough copy to understand it?**

The current website is not visually weak. It is visually ambitious. The problem is that several fashionable interaction and visual systems are stacked at the same time, which can make the result feel more like a "premium AI startup template" than a visual language uniquely earned by Polynovea.

Current implementation includes, among other things:

- a near-black base;
- violet and muted-gold accent system;
- fixed grid texture;
- multiple radial gradients;
- grain;
- animated gradient mesh;
- animated orbs;
- a full WebGL neural/network scene;
- bloom, vignette and noise;
- camera movement tied to scroll;
- cluster transitions;
- cursor parallax / cursor effects;
- liquid-glass cards;
- bento layouts;
- staggered entrance transitions;
- a fixed depth-scroll pager on desktop.

None of those elements is intrinsically wrong. The issue is **semantic dilution**: when everything moves, glows, refracts, floats and behaves like a "system", no single visual behaviour becomes recognisably Polynovea.

The redesign should therefore move from **more effects** to **fewer, ownable visual rules**.

## 22.1 Research principles

### A. Attractive interfaces matter, but aesthetics cannot compensate for weak usability

Nielsen Norman Group's aesthetic-usability research shows that attractive products are often perceived as easier to use, but the effect has limits: visual polish can hide minor usability problems, not severe information or interaction problems.

**Implication:** Polynovea should look exceptional, but the aesthetic system must strengthen the information architecture instead of becoming a layer visitors have to fight through.

### B. Motion attracts attention whether we want it to or not

NN/g's animation research stresses that peripheral motion automatically captures attention. Motion is most useful for feedback, state change, navigation continuity and spatial explanation; gratuitous animation can hijack attention.

**Implication:** motion on Polynovea should have a semantic job. A node moving, a line igniting, a section reconfiguring or a field changing state should represent a transition, relationship, signal or evidence state—not simply "make the site alive".

### C. Dark interfaces can be visually powerful but are not automatically the best reading environment

NN/g's review of dark-mode research notes that visual performance for people with normal or corrected-to-normal vision often favours light mode, while dark mode can still be aesthetically preferred and useful in particular contexts.

**Implication:** the cinematic dark identity can remain central, but long research articles, methods, publications and evidence-heavy content should have a deliberately optimised reading surface rather than forcing every page into the same luminous-black visual treatment.

### D. Users scan structure before reading detail

Eyetracking research shows strong layer-cake scanning: users jump between headings and subheadings and only read the body under a heading that appears relevant. Consistent hierarchy, whitespace and predictable placement increase scanning efficiency.

**Implication:** Polynovea pages should not depend on large walls of prose or equally weighted glass cards. Headings, status, evidence type, date/version, key result and next action should be visually scannable without reading every paragraph.

### E. Cards are not a universal design primitive

NN/g notes that cards consume more space and can make comparisons harder when the user must scan the same attribute across several options.

**Implication:** use cards for genuine conceptual grouping, not for every fact. Research results, evidence states, capability comparisons and product status may be stronger as structured rows, ledgers, timelines or tables.

### F. Accessibility is part of the visual system

WCAG 2.2 requires sufficient text contrast, visible keyboard focus, understandable headings/labels, predictable interaction and support for users affected by motion.

**Implication:** high contrast, focus states, reduced-motion behaviour and readable typography should be brand qualities, not compliance patches.

# 23. Recommended Polynovea visual identity direction

## 23.1 Design personality

The intended personality should sit between:

- **frontier research institution;**
- **serious systems / infrastructure company;**
- **experimental engineering lab;**
- **commercial software company that actually ships.**

It should not primarily resemble:

- a crypto landing page;
- a generic neon AI startup;
- a gaming interface;
- a glassmorphism component gallery;
- a conventional white SaaS template;
- a consulting-company brochure.

Working design phrase:

> **Experimental intelligence. Institutional discipline.**

A visitor should feel that Polynovea is exploring difficult systems, but also that those systems are measured, governed and documented.

## 23.2 Preserve the strongest current assets

Keep and strengthen:

- the Polynovea gold mark;
- near-black / obsidian as the primary environmental colour;
- restrained violet as the intelligence / active-state colour;
- muted gold as evidence / authority / validated-state colour;
- the current spatial depth idea;
- the idea of a network / state field;
- high-quality display typography;
- controlled grain;
- the sense that the interface exists inside a larger system rather than on a flat marketing canvas.

The redesign should **subtract generic treatments before adding new ones**.

## 23.3 Replace the generic "neural network" idea with a Polynovea Field

The present node-and-synapse scene is attractive, but "glowing neural network" is common AI visual language. Polynovea has enough conceptual material to own something more specific.

Working concept: **The Polynovea Field**.

It is not a literal HBIF scientific visualisation and must never be presented as one. It is a brand abstraction built from Polynovea's actual conceptual vocabulary:

- **observations / events** → small neutral points;
- **state / active behavioural dynamics** → violet structures;
- **evidence / validated transitions** → gold anchors or paths;
- **relationships** → restrained lines;
- **uncertainty** → diffuse envelopes / incomplete paths rather than decorative blur;
- **decisions / interventions** → visible branch or state transition;
- **outcomes / learning** → reconfiguration of the field.

This gives the background a semantic grammar.

The strongest version would not constantly move. It would remain calm, then reorganise only when the page meaning changes.

## 23.4 Background rules

### Home

The home page can remain immersive and cinematic.

Use:

- sparse dark field;
- one principal spatial structure at a time;
- low-frequency motion;
- meaningful camera movement;
- gold only at important evidence/state transitions;
- violet as active intelligence rather than as a permanent glow everywhere;
- large areas of visual silence.

Avoid:

- simultaneous grid + orbs + moving mesh + heavy bloom + cursor trail + high-frequency network pulses;
- excessive glass panels between the user and the field;
- continuous motion behind long paragraphs.

### Products

Products should feel operational rather than "scientific".

Background language:

- system topology;
- workflow connections;
- event/approval paths;
- clear module boundaries;
- restrained grid / control-plane cues.

The experience should feel like **complex operations becoming composed**, not like navigating a brain.

### Open Source

Open Source should feel inspectable and technical.

Background / graphic language:

- schemas;
- graph relationships;
- database/table structures;
- API/CLI/code fragments where useful;
- blueprint/grid geometry;
- version / release / contribution signals.

Less bloom. Less cinematic opacity. More precision.

### Research / HBIF

Research needs the most significant visual departure.

Use the dark Polynovea shell, but create a **research reading surface** inside it:

- warm off-white / parchment-neutral or high-contrast light panels for long-form reading;
- dark text for dense methods/results where appropriate;
- monospaced evidence labels;
- figures/diagrams with strong captions;
- citation / source / version rails;
- clear hypothesis → method → evidence → result → limitation structure.

The contrast between a dark "frontier" environment and a lighter "research paper / lab notebook" reading plane can become a signature rather than a loss of brand consistency.

This is also supported by dark-mode legibility research: the site can keep dark mode as an environmental identity without demanding inverted typography for every long-form research task.

### Company

Company pages should become more human.

Use real people, real work, diagrams, timelines, principles and evidence. Avoid making the company itself feel like another software module.

### Insights

Insights should behave like an editorial publication, not a marketing-card feed.

# 24. Interaction and motion system

## 24.1 Replace "motion everywhere" with motion roles

Define four permitted motion roles:

1. **Orient** — show where the user moved in the information architecture.
2. **Explain** — reveal a state change, relationship or transformation.
3. **Confirm** — acknowledge an interaction.
4. **Signal** — draw attention to a genuinely important state.

If a motion treatment serves none of these, remove it.

## 24.2 Reconsider full-home depth paging

The current desktop home maps multiple 100svh sections into a fixed depth stage, with only one pane readable at a time. The implementation is technically clever and already has mobile/reduced-motion fallbacks, but it imposes a strong browsing model on every desktop visitor.

Recommended direction:

- keep one or two signature depth transitions;
- use the spatial field to introduce Polynovea and possibly bridge into the three public surfaces;
- after the signature sequence, allow normal document scrolling for information-dense sections;
- do not require a camera journey to reach every FAQ, product fact or company detail.

This preserves the thing that makes the site memorable without turning ordinary information retrieval into a cinematic interaction.

## 24.3 Cursor effects

Cursor response should be exceptionally subtle or limited to the field itself.

The visitor should feel that the environment is responsive, not that the cursor is dragging an effect around the website.

## 24.4 Route transitions

Keep a restrained route transition if it preserves spatial continuity. The transition should never delay navigation or imply that the destination depends on WebGL.
