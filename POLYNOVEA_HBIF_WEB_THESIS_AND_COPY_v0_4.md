# 0. Purpose

This document develops the public-facing thesis for Polynovea's HBIF research presence from the actual research estate rather than from generic deep-tech positioning.

It is intended to guide:

- homepage research copy;
- `/research` copy and information hierarchy;
- `/architecture` explanation;
- animated research models;
- public research-result objects;
- evidence/status language;
- visual claim boundaries;
- later editorial and publication work.

The goal is not to make HBIF sound more certain than it is. The goal is to make the real research programme legible enough that the evidence is more interesting than the adjectives.

**Critical framing correction for v0.4:** the existing hospitality-heavy Layer 1 and BPM Layer 2 are the first substantial empirical research lineage. They are **not** assumed to survive into another domain. The three-layer architecture is itself a research programme under test: Infrakinetic can provide a new business-domain evidence environment, but whether the current Layer 1 constructs transfer, whether an analogous Layer 2 emerges at all, and whether any hospitality-derived mechanism survives into business are open questions.

# 2. Public architecture boundary

The current public architecture can still be explained as **three research layers**, but the layers must not be presented as if all three are already validated across domains.

The correct evidence state is:

1. **Layer 1 — Domain-specific observed reality / intelligence substrate**  
   The first substantial Layer 1 was built in hospitality from domain evidence, ontology, observations, interactions, operational signals and outcomes. Infrakinetic now creates a second, very different domain environment around business operations: events, workflows, approvals, people, commercial activity, finance, documents, decisions and outcomes. That gives HBIF a new domain to study; it does **not** mean the hospitality Layer 1 representation can simply be copied into business.

2. **Layer 2 — Experimental intermediate behavioural model**  
   In hospitality, BPM is the current Layer 2 experiment: an attempt to move from domain evidence toward state, dynamics, stability, anomaly, uncertainty and related quantities. The existence and shape of a useful Layer 2 in business is **not yet known**. Business may produce a different intermediate model, may preserve only some of the same concepts, or may falsify the usefulness of parts of the current Layer 2 altogether.

3. **Layer 3 — Domain-agnostic behavioural model**  
   Layer 3 remains a long-horizon convergence hypothesis. It can only be approached after multiple domains have been studied independently and we know which Layer 1 constructs, Layer 2 concepts, mathematical quantities, state representations or mechanisms actually survive translation and which do not.

This is therefore not yet a proven repeating stack of:

```text
Domain A: Layer 1 → Layer 2
Domain B: Layer 1 → Layer 2
Domain C: Layer 1 → Layer 2
                     ↓
                  Layer 3
```

The current research situation is closer to:

```text
HOSPITALITY
real domain evidence
→ Layer 1 built and tested
→ BPM / Layer 2 tested in that domain
→ candidate concepts and mechanisms
                                          ?  cross-domain survival is unknown
              /
             /
BUSINESS / INFRAKINETIC
real business operations
→ new domain evidence substrate
→ test which Layer 1 concepts transfer, fail or need replacement
→ investigate whether a useful Layer 2 emerges
→ compare only after the business evidence earns one

FUTURE DOMAINS
→ repeat the challenge independently

Only repeated survivors
→ candidate Layer 3 mechanisms
```

For the business domain, **Infrakinetic should be described as the operational substrate / observatory from which business-domain HBIF research can begin**, subject to explicit research permissions and governance. It should **not** yet be described as already having a defined HBIF Layer 2.

Likewise, BPM should be described as a **hospitality-domain Layer 2 experiment**, not as the generic HBIF Layer 2 and not as a template business is expected to reproduce.

The public governing question for the next research phase is therefore even more fundamental than simple cross-domain transfer:

> **Do the current Layer 1 and Layer 2 ideas survive contact with a genuinely different domain at all?**

Only after that question begins to resolve does the Layer 3 question become meaningful:

> **What remains when domain-specific representations and intermediate models are stripped away?**

The restricted frontier research described internally beyond this public three-layer programme is not ordinary website positioning.

# 3. The central web thesis

## 3.1 Programme thesis

> **HBIF is a cross-domain behavioural-intelligence research architecture. Each domain is modelled on its own terms first; only then are candidate state, dynamics and decision mechanisms challenged outside the domain that produced them.**

This is the governing frame for the website. The existing hospitality work is evidence of the method in action, not the definition of the programme.

The research register is explicit that every domain must preserve its own ontology and evidence boundary. The deeper objective is to test whether independently observed domain phenomena transfer, converge, contradict or falsify candidate mechanisms. A shared mechanism is therefore earned through comparison rather than assumed through vocabulary.

The long-horizon Layer 3 question is:

> **What remains of the behavioural structure when the domain-specific ontology is stripped away?**

A mechanism reaches that frontier only after independent evidence, translation testing, replication, falsification attempts, uncertainty calibration and documented boundary conditions.

## 3.2 State thesis

> **Within a real domain, behaviour is not well represented by a single static score. It is better treated as an evolving, evidence-conditioned state whose apparent structure can change with scale, context and time.**

This is strongly motivated by the current first-domain research estate. The next question is whether those state/dynamics ideas survive at all in a different domain. Business may require a different intermediate representation, preserve only part of the hospitality model, or provide evidence that some Layer 2 assumptions were domain-specific.

The existing work repeatedly motivates representing:

- state;
- stability;
- transition;
- heterogeneity;
- uncertainty;
- anomaly;
- temporal dynamics;
- evidence sufficiency.

The thesis is deliberately narrower than "we have mathematically solved human behaviour." The research record explicitly rejects that claim.

## 3.3 Method thesis

> **The interesting question is not whether a pattern can be found. It is whether the pattern survives.**

Survives what?

- a change in observational grain;
- a change in evidence quantity;
- alternative model constructions;
- hidden-state tests;
- time;
- different contexts;
- translation into a different domain;
- independent replication and falsification.

This should become the defining logic of the public Research surface.

## 3.4 Scale thesis — first-domain evidence

> **A pattern observed in an aggregate system is not automatically a law about an individual.**

The hospitality micro-vs-macro programme was an early direct test of this problem.

The research record reports that:

- review-level composite scores and aggregate venue representations can follow different statistical families;
- review-level data required more principal components than the aggregate venue representation;
- several category relationships that appear coupled at aggregate scale weaken sharply inside individual reviews;
- dense micro-level structure can coarse-grain into much simpler aggregate basins.

The defensible interpretation is not "human behaviour follows distribution X." It is:

> **Aggregation can create statistical structure that is not present at the raw-observation grain.**

That is a strong public case study because it demonstrates how HBIF challenges its own interpretation. It should be presented as a finding from the first proving domain, not as the endpoint of the framework.

## 3.5 Evidence thesis — first-domain evidence

> **Evidence sufficiency is part of the state, not a footnote attached to the answer.**

Multiple experiments in the current hospitality dataset converged around an evidence-stabilisation region near **9–10 observations**:

- macro phase/stability analysis near `t = 9`;
- threshold-sweep stability change near `t = 9`;
- review-level sequential evidence accumulation with median KL stabilisation around `N = 10`.

This must be described as a finding **in the current dataset and experimental construction**, not as a universal number for human behaviour.

A particularly useful nuance from the sequential evidence experiment is that only **29.8%** of the analysed venues had stabilised by `N = 9`, while the median stabilisation threshold was `N = 10`. The public visual should therefore show convergence as a distribution/process, not a magical hard cliff at nine reviews.

## 3.6 Hidden-state thesis — first-domain evidence

> **An average can be a poor description when the underlying system occupies more than one regime.**

The bimodality work recorded **29 venues** satisfying the chosen Hartigan dip criterion (`p < 0.05`) in the analysed corpus. The purpose was not merely to discover unusual distributions. It was to ask when a single aggregate posterior hides distinct behavioural regimes.

This became relevant to BPM's hidden-state concept. Again, the finding is useful because it produced a more disciplined state representation; it is not a claim that the same regime structure will appear identically in every future domain.

## 3.7 Research-to-system thesis

> **Research becomes useful when a candidate quantity survives long enough to change how a system is built.**

The current research register describes the bridge as:

```text
experiment
→ candidate quantity
→ calibrated computation
→ pipeline field
→ later domain use
```

BPM is the clearest current hospitality-domain Layer 2 experiment: several quantities moved from isolated experiments into a coherent behavioural state/dynamics pipeline inside that domain.

The next step is to challenge both the current Layer 1 abstractions and BPM Layer 2 against a genuinely different domain. Infrakinetic gives the programme a business-domain evidence environment for that test. We do not yet know whether business will produce an analogous Layer 2, a different intermediate structure, or no useful equivalent of some hospitality concepts at all.

This bridge should be highly visible on the website because it distinguishes Polynovea from both a pure research lab and a conventional software company.

# 4. A broader Polynovea thesis derived from the research

The following is **website synthesis**, not a direct quotation from a source:

> **Polynovea builds systems for environments where the state is changing, the evidence is incomplete, and decisions still have to be made.**

That sentence can connect the company-level story to both commercial systems and HBIF without pretending every product is an HBIF research instrument.

A second possible internal positioning line is:

> **Observe reality. Model state. Test what survives. Build only what the evidence earns.**

This is closer to the actual operating/research behaviour than generic "AI-powered intelligence" language.

Working copy:

> Once patterns appeared, the next job was not to celebrate them. It was to ask whether aggregation had manufactured them.

## 6.5 Section: “What changed with scale”

Public-safe finding objects from the first proving domain:

### Finding A — Distribution structure changed

**Observed**  
Review-level composite scores were reported as more log-normal under the tested fits, while aggregate venue representations appeared more Beta-like.

**What it suggests**  
Aggregation can create statistical structure not present in the raw observations.

**What it does not prove**  
That human behaviour universally follows either distribution.

### Finding B — Dimensionality compressed

**Observed**  
Review-level data required more principal components than the aggregate venue representation in the recorded experiment suite.

**What it suggests**  
Aggregation can reduce observable dimensionality.

**What it does not prove**  
That the compressed dimensions are universal human behavioural dimensions.

### Finding C — Coupling weakened at micro grain

**Observed**  
Several category relationships that appeared strongly coupled at venue scale had review-level normalised mutual information below approximately `0.10`.

**What it suggests**  
Some macro relationships may arise from context, repeated sampling, venue-type selection or aggregation rather than direct within-person coupling.

**What it does not prove**  
That those categories are psychologically independent in every context.

## 6.6 Section: “When does the evidence become stable enough to say anything?”

Working copy:

> A behavioural model should know not only what it thinks the state is, but how much evidence supports that state.

> Across several experiments in the first hospitality corpus, stability repeatedly changed in the region of roughly nine to ten observations. The review-level accumulation experiment reported a median stabilisation point of ten observations across 1,658 venues with at least 30 reviews. This is a dataset-conditioned result, not a universal behavioural constant.

Visual: animated evidence accumulation with uncertainty tightening gradually rather than a hard `N=9` switch.

## 6.7 Section: “One average can hide two realities”

Working copy:

> A mean is useful only when the underlying evidence is meaningfully described by one regime.

> In the current bimodality experiment, 29 venues met the selected Hartigan dip criterion. For those cases, one aggregate value could hide distinct behavioural regimes. That work became part of the motivation for making hidden-state warnings explicit in BPM.

Visual: one average line splits into two density peaks and a timeline alternates between regimes.

## 6.8 Section: “From experiment to a domain-specific Layer 2”

Working copy:

> The point of the experiment estate is not to decorate HBIF with physics vocabulary. Candidate quantities have to earn their way into the system.

```text
experiment
→ candidate quantity
→ calibration
→ state/dynamics computation
→ evidence boundary
→ later domain use
```

> BPM is the current hospitality-domain Layer 2 technical reference. It independently re-parses hospitality-domain evidence and computes state, dynamics, stability, anomaly and evidence-related quantities. It is calibrated against that research estate; it is not the universal Layer 2 of HBIF.

The public story should explicitly show that BPM is a **hospitality-domain Layer 2 experiment**. Infrakinetic should not be pre-labelled as having an equivalent Layer 2. Its job in the research story is to provide a new business-domain evidence environment in which the current Layer 1 and Layer 2 assumptions can be challenged.

## 6.9 Section: “Cross-domain is where HBIF starts becoming HBIF”

Working copy:

> A result from hospitality is not promoted because its vocabulary sounds general. It becomes a candidate for cross-domain challenge. Business evidence may preserve it, modify it, replace it or reject it.


> The long-horizon objective is not to make every domain agree. It is to learn which structures genuinely survive and which belong only to the environments that produced them.

That is the point at which the website should transition from historical evidence into current and future HBIF research questions.

# 7. `/architecture` copy thesis

The architecture page should explain the three public layers as a **research hypothesis with different evidence responsibilities**, not as a proven template repeated identically inside every domain.

## Layer 1 — Domain reality

Suggested copy:

> Every domain starts with its own reality: its language, entities, observations, workflows, decisions, actions, interventions and outcomes. Layer 1 names the domain-grounded evidence layer; it does **not** require the same ontology, primitives or representation to survive from one domain to another.

For the business domain, **Infrakinetic is the Layer 1 product surface**: the operational environment in which business events, workflows, decisions and outcomes can be represented structurally. Product operation and HBIF research rights remain separate; Layer 1 does not imply automatic research ingestion.

## Layer 2 — Domain-specific intermediate model

Suggested copy:

> Layer 2 is currently a research hypothesis born from the hospitality programme: can a higher-order intermediate model of state, dynamics, uncertainty and transition be constructed above domain evidence? BPM is one answer tested in hospitality. Whether business produces an analogous layer, a radically different one, or no useful equivalent of some BPM concepts is still open.

For business, Infrakinetic supplies the operational evidence environment from which we can investigate whether a meaningful intermediate Layer 2 exists at all. Its variables, mathematics and even its architecture must be earned by business-domain research rather than assumed from hospitality.

For hospitality, BPM is the current technical lineage for a hospitality-domain Layer 2. It is not the universal Layer 2 for HBIF.

## Layer 3 — What survives the domain

Suggested copy:

> Layer 3 begins only after enough independent domain evidence exists to determine what, if anything, survives beyond the source domain. It is the long-horizon domain-agnostic convergence target. A mechanism belongs here only after it survives cross-domain translation, independent testing, falsification attempts, uncertainty analysis and documented boundary conditions.

Hero question:

> **What remains when the domain-specific reality and the domain-specific model built on top of it are stripped away?**

A useful architecture visual should therefore be deliberately **asymmetric**. It should show what has actually been built versus what is still unresolved:

```text
HOSPITALITY                         BUSINESS / INFRAKINETIC
real observations                   real operational events
      ↓                                     ↓
Layer 1 constructs                        ?
      ↓                              test Layer 1 transfer
BPM / experimental Layer 2                ↓
      ↓                              preserve / modify / reject
candidate mechanisms                      ↓
      │                               Layer 2 ?
      │                          may emerge differently
      │                                     │
      └──────────── cross-domain challenge ─┘
                            ↓
                  what actually survives?
                            ↓
                 candidate Layer 3 only
                     where evidence earns it

FUTURE DOMAINS
→ repeat the challenge again
```

This is a stronger visual than three identical towers because the uncertainty is part of the research. The website should not visually pre-solve the very question HBIF is trying to answer.

# 8. Public research objects: the copy contract

## Research Result component

**Question**  
What was tested?

**Observed**  
What did the experiment actually report?

**Method / grain**  
At what observational level and under what construction?

What does the finding reasonably suggest?

**What it does not prove**

RUN + OUTPUTS / IMPLEMENTED / CALIBRATED / RESEARCH RESULT / etc.

**Scope**

**Artifact**  
Plot, table, experiment ID or public paper where available.

This component should become a signature part of the Polynovea visual system.

# 9. Animated model programme grounded in the actual research

The website should use animation to make the research logic understandable, not to imply that an abstract animation is a live scientific simulation.

Every model should carry a small label such as:

> **Conceptual visualisation based on the HBIF research architecture and recorded experiment structure — not a live behavioural inference.**

The animation programme should be split into two classes:

- **HBIF architecture models** — cross-domain, programme-level, not tied to hospitality;
- **research-lineage case studies** — concrete visualisations derived from the first hospitality-heavy experiment estate.

That separation prevents the historical data source from becoming the perceived identity of HBIF.

## 9.1 Model A — Domain Observatories → Cross-Domain Convergence

### Research basis

Directly from the current HBIF research map and cross-domain convergence protocol.

### Animation

1. Begin with three clearly different domain fields. They should not share raw labels, shapes or variables.
2. A new domain begins with its own raw reality, ontology, observations, workflows, decisions and outcomes. The animation should not assume these map cleanly onto the hospitality Layer 1 constructs; that mapping is itself part of the experiment.
3. In the hospitality lineage, an experimental Layer 2 formed above Layer 1 through BPM. In a new domain, the animation should leave this layer unresolved until evidence determines whether an analogous intermediate model appears.
4. Only after that do candidate structures rise out of the domain stack into a cross-domain comparison layer.
5. The comparison layer does **not** immediately merge them. Instead it attempts translation mappings.
6. Candidate mappings branch into **accept / modify / reject**.
7. Repeated survivors lose domain-specific labels and become progressively more abstract.
8. Only candidates with replicated evidence and visible boundary conditions enter the Layer 3 field.
9. Rejected or domain-bound mechanisms remain visible as legitimate research outcomes.

### Final copy

> **The domains are different on purpose.**

> HBIF begins with evidence inside a real domain. Hospitality produced one Layer 1 + experimental Layer 2 lineage. New domains are used to challenge that architecture itself. Layer 3 asks which mechanisms, if any, survive translation, replication and falsification strongly enough to become domain-agnostic.

This should become the signature public HBIF animation. It communicates the programme rather than one historical dataset.

## 9.2 Model B — Micro → Macro Emergence

### Classification

**First-domain research-lineage case study.**

### Research basis

- review-level and venue-level distribution fits differ;
- review-level representation requires more dimensions;
- micro category coupling is weaker;
- dense micro clustering coarse-grains into simpler venue-level basins.

### Animation

1. Begin with hundreds of neutral observation points, irregular and diffuse.
2. Select a few observations and expose their local state vectors.
3. Show low/weak coupling between several micro dimensions.
4. Aggregate points into entity containers.
5. As aggregation proceeds, the visual dimensionality compresses.
6. Simpler macro regions become visible.
7. A label appears: **“The aggregate structure was not identical to the structure of the observations that produced it.”**

### Final copy

> **Scale changes what the model sees.**

> In the first hospitality research estate, several mathematical properties changed between individual-review and venue-level representations. That is why HBIF keeps observational grain explicit rather than treating an aggregate pattern as an individual behavioural law.

## 9.3 Model C — Evidence Accumulation

### Classification

**First-domain research-lineage case study with a broader modelling principle.**

### Research basis

- macro stability change near t≈9;
- threshold sweep near t≈9;
- sequential KL median stabilisation near N=10;
- 29.8% stabilised by N=9 in the sequential experiment.

### Animation

1. Start at N=1 with broad uncertainty and a provisional state estimate.
2. Add observations one by one.
3. The estimate moves; uncertainty breathes and gradually narrows.
4. Around N≈9–10, many trajectories become more stable, but some continue moving.
5. Instead of a vertical magic boundary, show a distribution of stabilisation points.
6. At N=10 show a caption: **“Median stabilisation in this experiment — not a universal constant.”**

### Final copy

> **Confidence should change when the evidence changes.**

> Several experiments in the first-domain corpus converged around an evidence-stabilisation region near nine to ten observations. The broader HBIF principle is not the number itself; it is that evidence maturity belongs inside the state representation.

## 9.4 Model D — Hidden Regimes

### Classification

**First-domain research-lineage case study with a broader state-modelling question.**

### Research basis

- 29 bimodal venues under the chosen criterion;
- return maps and bimodality work;
- BPM hidden-state motivation.

### Animation

1. Show one aggregate score and an apparently clean mean.
2. Reveal the underlying observations.
3. The single distribution separates into two peaks.
4. A timeline reveals switching between states.
5. Cross out the single mean as a complete state description.

### Final copy

> **An average can hide two operating realities.**

> Bimodality tests in the first venue corpus identified cases where one aggregate value masked distinct behavioural regimes. That finding helped motivate explicit hidden-state handling rather than treating every average as a faithful state description.

## 9.5 Model E — Experiment → System → Cross-Domain Challenge

### Research basis

The canonical research loop and experiment-to-BPM bridge.

### Animation

```text
real domain question
→ experiment
→ measured quantity
→ calibration
→ evidence state
→ domain-specific Layer 2 use where justified
→ independent-domain challenge
→ accept / modify / reject
→ deeper abstraction only where earned
```

The key interaction is that some experiment nodes fail or remain exploratory rather than every branch reaching production. Likewise, a quantity that becomes useful inside one domain does not automatically become shared HBIF.

### Final copy

> **Not every interesting result becomes a system variable. Not every useful system variable becomes a cross-domain mechanism.**

> HBIF treats research, implementation and generalisation as separate evidence gates.

## Research-page safe with context

# 12. The strongest story hidden inside the current research

The most compelling public story is not any one number, equation, physics analogy, or even the first hospitality experiment estate.

It is the architecture of **progressive generalisation under evidence**:

```text
hospitality discovery domain
→ Layer 1 constructs emerge from hospitality evidence
→ BPM tests an experimental Layer 2
→ candidate mechanisms / quantities
→ challenge inside hospitality

new domain: business / Infrakinetic
→ collect genuinely different domain evidence
→ test whether current Layer 1 constructs still make sense
→ test whether BPM-like Layer 2 concepts survive, mutate or fail
→ allow new intermediate structure to emerge if the evidence demands it
→ cross-domain comparison only after comparable constructs exist
→ accept / modify / reject
→ repeat across further domains
→ domain-independent abstraction only where earned
```

The hospitality research is the first detailed example of the left-hand side of that process. Infrakinetic now gives us a business-domain environment to challenge it. We should not assume business will reproduce the same Layer 1 constructs or an equivalent BPM-like Layer 2:

```text
messy real-world evidence
→ structure appears
→ structure is challenged at another grain
→ some apparent laws weaken or disappear
→ evidence thresholds and hidden regimes are discovered
→ useful quantities are calibrated
→ selected quantities become state/dynamics machinery
```

That is the thesis the website should teach.

It communicates something much more credible than "we use AI to predict human behaviour."

It says:

> **We are trying to learn what can be known about behaviour inside a domain, what survives outside that domain, and where the model must stop.**

That line is a synthesis from the research estate and is a strong candidate for the public Research page.
